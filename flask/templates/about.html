Our Story

Founded in 2020, Lumina started with a simple idea: to bridge the gap between stunning design and seamless functionality. What began as a small team working out of a cramped garage has grown into a dynamic global agency.

We believe that great work comes from collaboration, curiosity, and a relentless pursuit of excellence. Every project we take on is an opportunity to push boundaries and build something unforgettable.
